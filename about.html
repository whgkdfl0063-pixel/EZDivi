About EZDivi

EZDivi started as a simple idea: most compound interest and dividend calculators online are either buried in spreadsheets, cluttered with unnecessary sign-ups, or too simplistic to actually reflect how real ETF portfolios behave.

This site brings together two tools — a compound interest calculator and an ETF dividend income calculator — in one clean, mobile-friendly page that works entirely in your browser. No accounts, no downloads, no data collection.

Why We Built This

Long-term investing is largely a game of patience, and it helps to actually see how a portfolio grows over years and decades rather than just reading about it in the abstract. The compound interest calculator is meant to make that growth curve tangible. The dividend calculator is meant to help you translate a percentage yield into an actual dollar figure — something that's often harder to picture than it should be, especially with newer covered-call and options-income ETFs that advertise very high headline yields.

Our Approach

  • We don't collect or store any of the numbers you enter — everything runs locally in your browser.
  • We try to flag risk clearly, especially around high-yield ETFs where a portion of the distribution may be return of capital rather than pure income.
  • We keep the reference data (ETF yields, tickers) as a starting point only — always verify current figures with the fund provider before making decisions.

Not Financial Advice

EZDivi is an educational tool, not a financial advisory service. Nothing on this site should be taken as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any specific investment. Always do your own research and, where appropriate, consult a licensed financial professional before making investment decisions.
